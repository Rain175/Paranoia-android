# Paranoia — The Ultimate Party Game (Android)

Paranoia is a party game built with **Kotlin** and **Jetpack Compose** for Android.

## Gameplay Overview

1. **Pass & Play / Party Room**: Players sit in a circle or join a party room.
2. **Secret Question**: The active Asker receives a secret spicy, hilarious, or dramatic question that only they can see.
3. **Out Loud Answer**: The Asker announces their answer *out loud* to everyone by naming one player.
4. **The Coin Flip**:
   - **HEADS (Cyan / Reveal)**: The secret question is revealed to everyone in the room!
   - **TAILS (Fuchsia / Secret)**: The question remains a secret forever. *Stay paranoid.*

## Features

- **Local Pass & Play**: Smooth device handoff with privacy shielding and "I'm Ready" security gate.
- **Party Room Mode**: Room creation with 4-letter join codes and live lobby synchronization.
- **Rich Question Categories**:
  - *Icebreaker & Chill* (Wholesome, safe for any group)
  - *Funny & Party* (Laughs, roasts & party chaos)
  - *Call-Out & Drama* (Juicy, dramatic, boundary-pushing)
  - *Spicy 18+* (Explicit, wild, adults only)
  - *Custom Questions* (Add and manage your own custom friend-group questions)
- **Interactive 3D Coin Flip**: Canvas-based physics animation with heads/tails suspense.
- **Paranoia Quotient & Round Log**: Track revealed vs secret ratio and game history recap.
- **Modern Material 3 Design**: Cyberpunk dark glassmorphism theme, dynamic colors, accessibility, and edge-to-edge support.
